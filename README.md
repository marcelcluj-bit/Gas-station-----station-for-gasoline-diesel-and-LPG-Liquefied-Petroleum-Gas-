# Global Highway Energy & Mobility Hub: Infrastructure & Web3 RWA Framework

## Overview
This repository provides a complete, open-source architectural blueprint and operational software framework for building modern **Highway Energy & Mobility Hubs**. Designed for turnkey execution, the project integrates civil infrastructure milestones, ultra-fast EV charging grids, smart retail automation, and Web3 Real World Asset (RWA) tokenization.

## Key Features & Construction Milestones

1. **Civil Infrastructure & Engineering:**
   - **Heavy-Duty Platform:** High-durability reinforced concrete maneuvering yards engineered for high-density heavy transport (trucks/TIRs).
   - **Underground Energy Vaults:** Modular, double-walled compartmentalized storage built to international ATEX/ISU engineering standards.
   - **Multi-Service Canopy & Store:** Integrated retail convenience store (250–400 sqm) with food court facilities and drive-thru capability.

2. **Smart Mobility & Energy Management:**
   - Dynamic load-balancing engine for ultra-fast EV charging networks (150–350 kW).
   - Rooftop Solar PV integration for zero-carbon auxiliary power generation.
   - 24/7 automated POS, fuel level telemetry, and fleet-card payment processing.

3. **Web3 RWA Tokenomics:**
   - On-chain fractional ownership model allowing global investors to participate in infrastructure financing (CapEx €1.88M–€2.90M).
   - Automated monthly yield disbursement powered by high-margin non-fuel retail, food court sales, and EV charging services via Smart Contracts.
